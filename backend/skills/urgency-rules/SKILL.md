# Urgency Rules
Severity scale: 1 = cosmetic, 3 = needs fix within a week, 5 = immediate danger.
- Hanging/exposed electric wires: 5
- Sewage overflow or large water leak: 4-5
- Pothole on main road: 3-4 (raise by 1 if raining)
- Garbage pile: 2-3 (raise by 1 if heat > 35C)
- Streetlight out: 2-3
Always give a one-sentence reason.
